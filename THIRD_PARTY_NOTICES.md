# Third-party notes
Original DSi RA Bridge code is MIT (LICENSE). No monitor/client source from
dscheevos, Bakkerrrs, nds-bootstrap, TWiLight Menu or xeRAbora is included.
The minimal Makefile uses standard devkitPro ds_rules conventions; no template is vendored.
Research source snapshots and submodule pins are documented, not redistributed.

rcheevos is MIT, NOT GPL. Its license is in docs/licenses/rcheevos-MIT.txt.
The optional engine build consumes a separately cloned official rcheevos commit.
A redistributed linked diagnostic must include the exact toolchain libraries' notices.
Do not redistribute a compiled .nds until devkitPro/libnds/libfat/default ARM7
license notices from the selected image are collected and checked.
nds-bootstrap/dscheevos/Bakkerrrs-derived patches require their GPL-3.0 source
and notices on distribution. This project currently ships no such patch.
Do not apply one component's license to all submodules or assets.
